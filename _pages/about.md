I am currently a President's Doctoral Scholar in the Department of Criminology at the [University of Manchester](https://www.socialsciences.manchester.ac.uk/criminology), where my doctoral work examines how access to natural environments shapes rates of self-harm and suicide among women incarcerated in Canada and the UK. I work at the intersection of **carceral geography**, environmental psychology, and critical feminist criminology, using mixed-methods that combine geospatial analysis with arts-based, diary-driven qualitative work — most recently presented at the [6th International Conference for Carceral Geography](https://carceralgeographies.co.uk/carcerality-borders-and-mobility) in Trier and as an invited speaker for [ICPA's Planning and Design Network](https://icpa.org/events/past-events/icpa-planning-and-design-network.html).

My research foregrounds **person-centred, rights-based prison design**, challenging traditional carceral norms in favour of policy that treats dignity, resilience, and wellbeing as design requirements rather than afterthoughts. I'm the chair and founder of the [ECR PRISON network](https://ecrprison.com) (Early Career Prison Researchers and International Scholars Organised Network) and a member of the Carceral Geography Working Group at the RGS-IBG.

Before Manchester, I completed an [MRes in Criminological Research](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/courses/criminological-research-mres) at the University of Liverpool (Pass with Distinction), where I also represented postgraduate researchers in the School of Law and Social Justice, and a [BA (Hons) in Criminal Justice](https://www.mtroyal.ca/ProgramsCourses/FacultiesSchoolsCentres/Arts/Departments/EconomicsJusticePolicyStudies/DegreePrograms/BachelorofArts-CriminalJustice/index.htm) at Mount Royal University (GPA 3.98/4.0), graduating with the Centennial Gold Medal for the Faculty of Arts and a [Top 30 Under 30 award] (https://top30under30.acgc.ca/2023-recipients/) from the Alberta Council for Global Cooperation. Prior to returning fully to research, I led a small technology-and-social-impact nonprofit in Calgary as Interim Executive Director of the [Centre for Social Impact Technology](https://www.centreforsocialimpacttech.ca/), served as President of my university's [Golden Key](https://www.goldenkey.org/) chapter, and was a Fellow of the [Royal Society for Arts, Manufactures and Commerce](https://www.thersa.org/) from 2022 to 2025.

I'm also a Red Seal baker in my previous life, a fact that comes up more often in conversation than you'd expect from a prison researcher. Based in Manchester — feel free to get in touch.
